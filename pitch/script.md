# Pitch script: 3 minutes + 2 minutes Q&A

Before you start: demo laptop, app on **Supplier ops**, **Reset demo** pressed, browser zoom 125%, live AI OFF.
The phone has the Buyer phone view open (same Wi-Fi, laptop's Network URL). **The public link is on the closing slide
only:** the stage demo runs on the laptop's own copy, so judges trying the public app can't touch it. Don't run the
demo itself on the public app (plan B) while the link is on screen.

Never say a number that isn't on screen. Every number below is from the app.

## 0:00–0:30 · C: the buyer and the shipment at risk (no clicks)
> "£60 on Monday beats £1,650 on Wednesday. Here's why.
> It's Monday afternoon at Apex Timing, a small supplier in Milton Keynes. Meet Sarah, the Logistics Manager. Their truck to Monza leaves at 7pm.
> One timing unit is still on the test bench, with about a one-in-three chance it fails and misses the truck. The
> garage needs it by Thursday 18:00. Today they'd wait, and if it fails, pay £1,650 for an emergency van: a whole
> vehicle driving about 1,400 km for one crate."

## 0:30–2:00 · B clicks, A narrates
| Click | Say |
|---|---|
| **Run** | "Safety Car reads the carriers' messages: two emails, a WhatsApp and a PDF quote. This is a replayed run, so it can't fail on stage. Every number links back to the exact words it came from." |
| Agent log: SwiftVan line (pause 1 s) | "One carrier's message tells 'your system' it can approve £500. The agent ignores it: spending limits come only from the buyer's own policy." |
| AeroDash "32kg" | "Small print: 'max 32kg per piece'. Our crate is 45 kg, so the air courier is out." |
| £60 receipt | "The best move is a £60 hold on a truck already going to Monza. That's inside Sarah's £100 policy, so the agent paid. The receipt shows what's paid for, and what isn't: the £340 transport." |
| **Replay £120** → C taps **Approve** on the phone | "Now the carrier raises the fee to £120. That's over the limit, so the agent can't pay. It asks Sarah, with the amount and the reason." … "Approved: receipt and ledger." |
| Paperwork ✓ → **Bench test failed** → C approves £340 | "The unit failed. Confirming costs £340, so Sarah approves that too, and the carrier confirms: collection Tuesday 20:00, at Monza Thursday 09:00." |

If you're over 1:30 in rehearsal, skip the £60 run and say "At £60 it books on its own, inside the limit." Keep the
£120 approval and the confirmation.

## 2:00–3:00 · C, with the impact card on screen
> "One dedicated cross-border trip avoided. If the unit is late, it costs £460 instead of £1,650. We arrive 19 hours
> after the van would, but still 9 hours before the deadline. Our modelled estimate is about 18 kg of CO2e
> instead of 356, using UK Government factors. That's with the van's empty return left out, which flatters the
> van." *(B flips "Count the van's empty return trip": ≈356 → ≈712 kg.)*
>
> "Carriers pay us to fill space that would travel empty. Suppliers pay nothing extra, and they get the same
> decision every race weekend.
>
> In Stockholm we'd test one thing: will suppliers set a standing spend policy and let the agent book holds
> without asking? Five suppliers, one race weekend, SolvaPay buyer accounts. We pass if at least four keep their
> policy for the next race, the carriers honour every hold, and no confirmed shipment misses its deadline. For
> SolvaPay, it's the first real data on how much spending authority businesses will hand to an agent, which is
> what your buyer accounts sell."
